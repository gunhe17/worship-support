I'll compare local and cheap cloud Korean STT options for sung lyrics and speech, using X and the web, and I'll cite every number. Starting with published benchmarks, licenses, prices, and what Korean developers have said in 2025–2026.For spoken Korean on an M3 with 24GB, use a Korean-tuned Whisper medium locally. For the lowest API bill, use Groq Whisper turbo. For sung worship lyrics, separate the vocals first and align a known lyric text. No published Korean singing error rate exists for any model below.

The only independent Korean comparison that puts these open models on the same sets is [seastar105’s April 2026 bench](https://github.com/seastar105/oss-asr-korean-benchmark) (utterances ≤30s; not songs, not long-form). Commercial numbers come from [ReturnZero’s own bench](https://github.com/rtzr/Awesome-Korean-Speech-Recognition) and are not comparable to that table.

## Recommendations

**(1) Local, spoken Korean.** `seastar105/whisper-medium-komixv2` through [mlx-whisper](https://github.com/ml-explore/mlx-examples) or [whisper.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/whisper.cpp) (Metal). Average character error rate (CER) **7.05%** on that 8-set bench, and **8.91% / 8.31%** on KsponSpeech eval-clean / eval-other, ahead of stock large-v3 (**14.16% / 13.08%**). A medium Whisper fits easily in 24GB. Stock **large-v3** is the runner-up (**7.83%** average) and slightly better on the call-center set (**5.34%** vs **5.78%**). Skip the 0.6B Qwen3-ASR for phone audio: **59.62%** CER on that call-center set.

**(2) Cheapest API.** [Groq `whisper-large-v3-turbo`](https://console.groq.com/docs/model/whisper-large-v3-turbo) at **$0.04 per hour**. Quality is stock turbo: **9.36%** average CER on the same open bench, not a Korean specialist. Groq large-v3 is **$0.111/hour**. If you need the best published Korean speech accuracy and will pay more, [ReturnZero sommers](https://developers.rtzr.ai/docs/en/pricing/) is **₩1,000/hour** for the first 1,000 hours (their bench: **5.91%** average CER). A USD conversion was not checked on 2026-09-28, so that dollar figure is **UNVERIFIED**. It is still several times Groq’s price.

**(3) Lyrics alignment.** Worship songs usually have a known text, so this is forced alignment, not recognition. [Qwen3-ForcedAligner-0.6B](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-ASR) is the only open aligner with a Korean number: **37.2 ms** average absolute shift on speech (lower is better). WhisperX has no Korean number in that table. The aligner card says **speech**, not singing, so sung accuracy is **UNVERIFIED**. Practical path: split the vocal (Demucs or UVR), align the published lyrics, then fix by hand. If the lyric text is unknown, run **Qwen3-ASR-1.7B** after vocal separation. It is the only open model that documents singing and songs with backing tracks, but every published singing score is Chinese or English, not Korean.

## Worship pipeline

1. Split the lead vocal from the band. Full mixes are where every general ASR model collapses, and Whisper is not even scored on full songs with BGM in the Qwen report.
2. If the lyrics are the published worship text, align them with Qwen3-ForcedAligner-0.6B (Korean, Apache-2.0). Expect to repair melisma, held notes, and choir unisons. Sung alignment quality is **UNVERIFIED**.
3. If you must discover the lyric text, transcribe the isolated vocal with Qwen3-ASR-1.7B and with komixv2 or large-v3, and keep only the lines they agree on. One Korean user already saw Qwen invent sentences on speech, which is worse on music.
4. Do not send the mix to Deepgram Nova-2, MMS, Moonshine, Canary/Parakeet open weights, or Qwen3-ASR-0.6B.
